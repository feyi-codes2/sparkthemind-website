# SparkTheMind Website Design Direction

## Three Possible Directions

### Theme Name: Editorial Hope
Very Brief Intro: A warm, editorial nonprofit identity that pairs confident blue with paper-like neutrals, human photography, and a journal-inspired rhythm. It feels credible, optimistic, and grounded in real community work.

Probability: 0.07

### Theme Name: Bright Field Notes
Very Brief Intro: A lively, youth-centred direction using open space, hand-drawn marks, and bright accents to make the organisation feel active, accessible, and encouraging. It prioritises energy and participation.

Probability: 0.04

### Theme Name: Quiet Momentum
Very Brief Intro: A calm, premium system built around deep ink, electric blue, and cinematic photography. It communicates focus, ambition, and a serious commitment to long-term educational change.

Probability: 0.08

## Chosen Direction: Editorial Hope

### Design Movement
Contemporary editorial nonprofit design, influenced by independent magazines and modern social-impact annual reports. The visual language should feel human, intentional, and documented rather than corporate or overly playful.

### Core Principles
1. **Hope with evidence:** Pair optimistic language with clear figures, event records, and tangible programme descriptions.
2. **Human scale:** Use close, dignified photography and generous text blocks that let girls and their learning environments remain the focus.
3. **Editorial rhythm:** Alternate full-bleed moments, offset text columns, framed statistics, and compact programme cards instead of repeating identical centered sections.
4. **Confident simplicity:** Keep the interface accessible and direct, with a small set of repeated actions and no decorative clutter.

### Color Philosophy
SparkTheMind blue is the signature signal of possibility and forward motion. It should appear in moments that ask for attention: navigation action, primary buttons, numbers, and section anchors. Warm parchment and soft cream surfaces prevent the blue from feeling clinical and create a sense of printed field notes. Ink-black text adds seriousness and readability; coral is reserved for small moments of human warmth, never for primary actions.

### Layout Paradigm
Use an asymmetric editorial composition with a strong left rail for section labels, oversized headings, and image blocks that intentionally break the container edge. The homepage should feel like a sequence of confident spreads: an image-led hero, an offset mission statement, a staggered programme trio, an impact band, and a full-width invitation to participate.

### Signature Elements
1. Small uppercase section labels with a thin rule, like chapter markers in a report.
2. A blue “spark” motif made from a four-point star mark used beside key headings and in the logo.
3. Framed impact statistics that combine an oversized number with a short, human sentence.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like a page turning or a marker being placed: quick, clear, and purposeful. Hover states should lift cards slightly, reveal an accent line, or shift the image crop. Navigation should remain easy to understand and never hide the next action behind novelty.

### Animation
Use restrained entrance motion: text rises 18px and fades in over 500ms; image blocks reveal with a subtle scale from 1.04 to 1.0; cards stagger by 60ms. Buttons respond with a 160ms scale and shadow change. Respect prefers-reduced-motion by disabling reveals and image transforms while preserving opacity and focus states.

### Typography System
Use **DM Serif Display** for large editorial headlines and **Manrope** for navigation, body copy, labels, and buttons. Headlines should be large, compact, and slightly irregular in rhythm; body copy should sit between 17–19px on desktop with comfortable line height. Uppercase labels should use 11–12px tracking of 0.16em.

### Brand Essence
SparkTheMind is an education-focused organisation for girls, schools, and supporters who believe opportunity should not depend on preventable barriers. It is different because it combines direct school outreach with encouragement and practical support.

Personality adjectives: **hopeful, grounded, determined**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines should be confident and emotionally clear. CTAs should be active and specific. Microcopy should sound welcoming, respectful, and community-minded without becoming sentimental or vague.

Example lines:

> Every girl has a future worth investing in.

> Bring encouragement closer to the girls who need it.

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a custom wordmark treatment with “Spark” set in a confident serif and “TheMind” in a clean sans-serif, paired with a standalone four-point spark icon. The icon should work independently as a favicon and social avatar; the wordmark should never rely on a default browser font.

### Signature Brand Color
**Spark Blue — `#0A57FB`**. This is the ownable action color: immediate, optimistic, and recognisable across the website.

## Style Decisions

- Use editorial asymmetry over generic centered blocks.
- Use cream and ink surfaces alongside Spark Blue to create warmth and contrast.
- Use real event and school photography as soon as approved assets are available; generated imagery will serve as a temporary visual foundation for the first build.
- Keep the verified impact figure at **500+ girl students reached** until the organisation confirms whether 1,000 is accurate.
- Keep Mission as the emotional “why,” Programmes as the practical “what,” and Impact as the evidence.
